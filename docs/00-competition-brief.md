# GenHacks 2026 — Competition Brief

Source: https://genhacks-open.devpost.com/ (rules read 2026-11-19)
Status: **registered**

## Format
- Fully online, global. Open to students aged 13+, any level of school.
- Solo or a team of **any size**.
- Organizers: Shail Patel & Sharaf Karim. Contact: projectdextera@gmail.com

## Dates (Eastern Time)
- Build window opens: Nov 1, 2026
- **Submission closes: Dec 26, 2026 at 12:00 AM ET**
  - NOTE: 12:00 AM on Dec 26 = the *start* of Dec 26, i.e. **end of Dec 25**.
  - Treat the real working deadline as **Dec 24, 2026** to leave a safety buffer.
- Late submissions are not judged.

## Tracks (pick exactly one at submission)
1. **Healthcare & Environmental** — improves health/wellbeing or the environment
2. **Robotics & IoT** — uses hardware
3. **AI, Agentic Systems, Cybersecurity, Dev Tools**
4. **Open** — anything else

Organizers may move a project to a better-fitting track.

## What must be submitted
- [ ] Devpost project page: name, what it does, how it was built, tools used
- [ ] Demo video, **max 3 minutes**, on YouTube or Vimeo, **embedding enabled**, showing it working
- [ ] Public code link (e.g. GitHub). Hardware projects also need photos/video of the device
- [ ] Chosen track
- [ ] All team members added on Devpost
- [ ] (Optional) Opt in to "Best Use of Deployxa" if deployed there

## Judging criteria — equally weighted, 25% each
| Criterion | What they ask |
|---|---|
| Impact (25%) | Does it solve a real problem for the people/area its track covers? |
| Technical execution (25%) | Does it work, and how much did the team build *during* the hackathon? |
| Creativity (25%) | How original is the idea or approach? |
| Presentation (25%) | Do the video and project page clearly show what it does and how? |

Judges score within each track, then the top projects are re-judged overall for the top 3.

### Implication of the 25/25/25/25 split
Presentation is worth as much as the code. The 3-minute video and the project page
are 25% of the score on their own — budget real time for them, not the last hour.

## Rules that affect how we work
- **Must be started after Nov 1, 2026.** Projects begun earlier are disqualified.
- Open-source libraries, APIs, templates and **AI tools are allowed** — but must be
  listed in the project description.
- No copying someone else's work.
- You keep all rights; organizers may use name/description/images/video to promote.
